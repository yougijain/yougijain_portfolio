Aug 2026 Game Theory / EV Engine

Poker Trainer

A no-limit hold'em trainer for 2 to 6 handed cash games. You enter the spot and it returns the GTO baseline, the EV of every alternative in big blinds, and the adjustment for the players at the table. Equity is enumerated exactly when the work fits in 400K hand evaluations and estimated by Monte Carlo otherwise. Exploits are computed by changing the model of an opponent, such as how much equity they need before they call, and re-running the same EV comparison. Bluffs stay capped at the b/(p+b) ratio unless a read says the opponent folds too much.

Jun 2026 Experimentation / Causal

When Average Effects Lie

Turns a 64,000-customer randomized email test into a targeting plan. The Women's email raises site visits by 4.5 points on average, but that splits into 7.3 points for past buyers of women's merchandise and 1.1 for everyone else. After balance checks, Lin-adjusted effects, and FDR-corrected interaction tests, I compared T-, S-, and DR-learners by cross-fitted Qini. The chosen policy is worth $16.95 more per 1,000 customers (95% bootstrap interval $2.99 to $30.60) and sends 41% fewer emails.

Sep 2026 Live ML / Calibrated Risk Model

NYC Collisions

A gradient-boosted model estimates how likely each of 637K NYC crashes was to injure someone. It sees what was involved in a crash but not where it happened, so each intersection can be compared with what its own crashes predict. The model is fit on 2020–2023, calibrated on 2024, and tested on 2025 onward. A backtest against a permutation null shows the ranking does predict later excess injuries, though about as well as a plain injury-rate sort. New data and a retrain land every Monday.

Sep 2026 In progress Real-Time CV / Forecasting

Hall Check

Counts the people in line at five public UMass dining-hall cameras, one frame every two minutes. YOLO11n runs on a CPU and counts people whose feet fall inside a queue polygon, and the frame is never stored: CI fails the build on any pixel-writing call or a detector save flag that isn't pinned off. The evaluation was fixed before any data came in. The accuracy report fails until 150+ blind hand counts cover every hall, meal, and lighting condition, and the 30-minute forecast fails unless it beats persistence and same-slot baselines. A daily drift check tells a moved camera from an outage.

Sep 2026 Live Applied Statistics

Crate Intelligence

Product analytics on Spotify listening history, pointed at DJ questions and built on DuckDB. Shuffle causes more skips and also more key clashes, so I tested harmonic mixing with a Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel test stratified on shuffle. On the bundled synthetic sample, pooling the data would overstate the clash effect by 38%. Tracks are ranked by Wilson lower bounds, and the beam-search set builder is benchmarked against greedy and 200 random orderings. None of my own listening data is in the repo.

02 Notes
  • How many hands until a win rate means something? Oct 2026 A 5 bb/100 edge needs about 124,000 hands before its 95% interval clears zero, and about 254,000 to clear it 80% of the time. With a calculator for your own numbers.
  • Sizing a bankroll when you don't know your edge Oct 2026 24 buy-ins give a 5 bb/100 winner a 5% risk of ruin. If the true edge is half that, the risk is 22%, and sizing for the larger edge means zero long-run growth.
